# MechWarrior 4: Vengeance — Extended Campaign
## Mission Prompts: Operations 8 & 9

**How to use:** Each mission below is a self-contained prompt. Feed one block at a time into your mission generator. The blocks continue directly from the end of Operation 7 and assume the canon branch (Operation 6, Mission 4b — the armory was captured, Joanna was lost, and Karl Sontag deserted). If your playthrough saved Joanna, apply the Branch Notes at the end of this document.

### Continuity snapshot (start of Operation 8)
Ian Dresari is now Duke of Kentares IV. Roland, William, Kulin, Burke, Castro, and Peter are dead; in the canon branch, so is Joanna, and Karl Sontag has deserted. The Omega Lance roster is Ian Dresari, Casey Nolan, Jen McQuarrie, Jules Gonzales, and Terra Risner, with Damon Squire missing in action after going AWOL (usable later as a rescue hook or replacement pilot). The Lyran occupation is broken, but the planet is not at peace: cut-off garrisons refuse to surrender, deserters have turned raider, a naval squadron survives at Argonne, and nobles who backed William are running for cover.

## Operation 8 — "Ashes of Victory"
*Theme: winning the war was the easy part. Omega Lance mops up the occupation's remnants while Ian's rule begins to harden.*

### Mission 1 — Secure the Starport
**Briefing:** Days after Roland's death, intercepted traffic reveals a Steiner rearguard under Kommandant Elise Voss holding Kentares City's civilian starport. Her troops are loading a grounded Overlord-class DropShip, the *Valkyrie's Wrath*, with the planetary treasury, looted Dresari heirlooms, and — most dangerous of all — Roland's intelligence archives naming every collaborator and every resistance sympathizer on Kentares. Omega Lance must take the starport before the ship lifts.
**Objectives:** Primary — breach the perimeter turret grid; disable the *Valkyrie's Wrath* by destroying its drive housings before the launch countdown expires; eliminate Voss's command lance. Secondary — stop the cargo crawlers before they reach the loading ramp (preserves the archives); keep the passenger terminal, now a civilian shelter, undamaged.
**Terrain:** Urban starport — ferrocrete landing pads, fuel farms (destructible, chain explosions), hangars, control tower.
**Opposition:** A recon lance (Osiris, Uller, Raven) plus Voss's command lance (Zeus, Catapult, two Bushwackers), anti-air tanks, and the turret grid.
**Events:** A visible launch timer drives the pacing. Igniting the fuel farm near the shelter triggers a civilian-casualty warning and mission failure risk. Mid-mission, Voss offers to hand over the archives in exchange for free passage; Ian, hardened by Joanna's death, refuses — a first hint of the man he is becoming.
**Success/Failure:** Success — DropShip grounded, Voss defeated. Failure — the ship launches or the shelter is destroyed.

### Mission 2 — The Argonne Remnant
**Briefing:** The naval base at Argonne never surrendered. Its last destroyer, the *Vigilant*, has been shelling fishing towns along the Meridian Coast and raiding for fuel, screened by fast patrol boats and shore batteries. The boat crews who ran the Vale evacuation have asked their new Duke to finish what Omega Lance started at Vale.
**Objectives:** Primary — destroy three coastal battery sites to open the harbor approach; sink the patrol boat screen; sink the *Vigilant* before it clears the harbor mouth into deep water. Secondary — capture the Argonne fuel depot intact for the new government.
**Terrain:** Rocky coastline and shallow bays (favoring lighter, faster 'Mechs), harbor fortifications, the base itself.
**Opposition:** Shore turrets, hovercraft patrols, fast patrol boats, and a garrison lance (Uziel, Shadow Cat, Cougar, Catapult).
**Events:** The *Vigilant* gets under way roughly eight minutes in. A resistance spotter, callsign "Kingfisher," marks targets from a fishing trawler. A storm front rolls in late in the mission, cutting visibility for the final naval engagement. Jen McQuarrie jokes that she is going to start demanding a navy pension.
**Success/Failure:** Success — the *Vigilant* on the bottom of the harbor. Failure — it escapes to open ocean.

### Mission 3 — Wolves of the Tundra
**Briefing:** Relief convoys into the Elysium tundra are being hit by raiders flying no colors — Lyran deserters who looted a repair depot during the collapse. Their trail leads back to the old recon outpost Omega Lance destroyed during the first landing, now rebuilt as a pirate den.
**Objectives:** Primary — escort the relief convoy through Widow's Pass; track the raiders back to the outpost; destroy the pirate lance and their salvage stores; free the captured convoy drivers. Secondary — recover the stolen medical supplies intact.
**Terrain:** Arctic tundra, ice fields, and a frozen lake with thin-ice zones that crack under 'Mechs above 80 tons.
**Opposition:** A patchwork salvage lance (a battered Vulture, a one-armed Thor, two Ullers, a Raven) plus armed trucks and technicals.
**Events:** The convoy is ambushed at the midpoint of the pass. The pirate leader, "Blackard," taunts Omega Lance over an open channel. The mission's tone makes the point that the war is over but the suffering is not.
**Success/Failure:** Success — convoy through, outpost burned, drivers freed. Failure — convoy destroyed.

### Mission 4 — The Duke's Justice
**Briefing:** Intercepts reveal that Baron Aldous Tremont, a Kentaran noble who backed William, is smuggling Leutnant-Colonel Marta Hess — Roland's chief of intelligence at the university headquarters — to a hidden airstrip in the Meer highlands. Hess carries the only surviving copy of Roland's collaborator files. Joanna would have wanted a trial. Ian wants results.
**Objectives:** Primary — intercept Tremont's convoy before it reaches the airstrip; destroy the escort lance; prevent Hess's shuttle from launching. Secondary (player choice) — capture Hess alive by disabling her command van, or simply destroy it. Capture preserves the files; destruction is faster and safer.
**Terrain:** Highland switchbacks, terraced farms, river fords, a concealed airstrip with flak emplacements.
**Opposition:** Tremont's household guard lance (Argus, two Uziels, Chimera), armored cars, and airstrip defenses.
**Events:** If Ian orders the van destroyed with Hess aboard, Terra Risner objects on an open channel — a character beat that foreshadows the Duke's hardening. Tremont surrenders once his guard lance is destroyed.
**Success/Failure:** Success — Hess and the files do not leave the planet, by whichever method. Failure — the shuttle launches.

### Mission 5 — The Last Garrison
**Briefing:** Hauptmann Dieter Falk, the last surviving officer of Vonda Castro's elite guard, holds the mountain fortress of Karst Redoubt with a company of die-hards — and two hundred political prisoners taken in the occupation's final days. He demands safe passage offworld. Duke Ian's answer is Omega Lance.
**Objectives:** Primary — escort resistance commandos to the redoubt's turret control bunker; once the turrets fall, breach the main gate; defeat Falk's guard lances; secure the prisoner blocks before Falk's men can act against the hostages. Secondary — keep all commandos alive.
**Terrain:** Alpine fortress — a kill-box approach road, gatehouse, inner bailey, and a cellblock plateau.
**Opposition:** A dense turret grid, two guard lances (a mix of Loki, Thor, Vulture, and Mauler), and Falk himself in an Atlas.
**Events:** A timed hostage phase begins the moment the gate falls. Falk challenges Ian by name over comms, calling him "the butcher of Kentares"; Ian's reply differs by story branch. Victory ends the operation with a newscast epilogue: the last organized Lyran force on Kentares surrenders — and rumors surface that the Archon has quietly hired outside help.
**Success/Failure:** Success — fortress taken, prisoners alive. Failure — hostages lost or the commando team wiped out.

## Operation 9 — "The Archon's Answer"
*Theme: Katrina Steiner-Davion, bogged down in the wider civil war, cannot spare line regiments — so she quietly contracts the Gray Talons, a hard-bitten mercenary combined-arms regiment, to make an example of Kentares. Their DropShips ground on the Hardra Peninsula, where the resistance war began.*

### Mission 1 — Eyes on the Drop
**Briefing:** Orbital watch reports multiple DropShips grounding near the abandoned firebases on the Hardra Peninsula. Before the Duke commits his forces, he needs to know what he is facing. Omega Lance inserts by night for a reconnaissance in force.
**Objectives:** Primary — scout and image three landing zones (drive-through NAV point recon); plant target beacons on the Talon fuel dumps; exfiltrate to the extraction point. Secondary — remain undetected until the third beacon is planted. Stealth is encouraged but not required; detection turns the exfiltration into a running fight.
**Terrain:** Desert night map — dunes, dry wadis, and the ruins of the old resistance firebases.
**Opposition:** Talon picket lances (Cougars, Shadow Cats, and sensor-equipped Ravens), searchlight towers, and patrol VTOLs.
**Events:** If detected, reaction lances converge and the extraction shifts to a hot LZ. Intercepted Talon traffic introduces their commander, Colonel Katherine Vane — a cashiered former Lyran regular with a grudge and a contract.
**Success/Failure:** Success — three zones imaged, beacons planted, lance extracted. Failure — Omega Lance destroyed before exfiltration.

### Mission 2 — Cut the Chain
**Briefing:** The Talons are building up for a drive on Jeteel. Their entire supply chain crosses the Hardra river gorge on a single heavy bridge. Omega Lance will escort combat engineers to drop the span and burn the forward depot — the same play that once saved the satellite technicians.
**Objectives:** Primary — escort the engineer convoy to the gorge; defend the bridge site while the charges are set (timed defense); destroy the forward supply depot on the far bank; withdraw before Talon heavy elements arrive. Secondary — all engineer vehicles survive.
**Terrain:** River gorge, canyon roads, and a fortified depot compound.
**Opposition:** The depot garrison (Bushwackers, an Argus, a Catapult) with a late-arriving heavy reaction lance (Thor, Loki, Mauler).
**Events:** The bridge detonation plays as a set-piece. Afterward Vane radios Ian directly — professional, without hatred: nothing personal, the Archon simply pays better.
**Success/Failure:** Success — bridge down, depot burned. Failure — charges never set, or the engineers are wiped out.

### Mission 3 — The Line at Jeteel
**Briefing:** The Talons' main body is advancing on Jeteel, the resistance's old staging capital, and civilian evacuation is under way. Omega Lance, with Denali Lance and militia armor in support, must hold until the transports are clear.
**Objectives:** Primary — hold three defensive NAV sectors against successive assault waves; protect the evacuation column; keep at least one artillery battery alive through the final wave. Secondary — no structures lost in the old resistance headquarters compound.
**Terrain:** Town outskirts, a ridgeline, and an artillery firebase.
**Opposition:** Three escalating waves — a recon probe, the main body (Vultures, Thors, a Zeus), and a breakthrough element led by Vane's executive officer in a Daishi.
**Events:** Denali Lance returns as an allied unit. Ammunition trucks resupply Omega Lance between waves. If the artillery battery survives, it becomes available as fire support in the finale of Mission 4.
**Success/Failure:** Success — evacuation completed, line held. Failure — the column is destroyed or all three sectors fall.

### Mission 4 — Duel at the Palace Gates
**Briefing:** Bloodied at Jeteel, Vane gambles everything on a decapitation strike, driving straight for Kentares City and the Dresari palace. Ian meets her in the same shattered streets where he killed Roland — and William.
**Objectives:** Primary — destroy the Talon spearhead in the palace approaches; protect the palace turret control center (the reverse of Operation 7); defeat Colonel Vane's command lance. Endgame — Vane jams all communications and challenges Ian to single combat in the palace plaza.
**Terrain:** Ruined capital streets, the plaza, and the palace gates.
**Opposition:** Spearhead lances (Mauler, Awesome, Loki, Catapult) and Vane herself in a Daishi.
**Events:** If the Jeteel battery survived, one artillery support call is available before the jamming begins. The duel deliberately mirrors the William fight: no allied support, open-channel dialogue, one victor.
**Epilogue:** With Vane dead, the Gray Talons invoke a breach clause and withdraw offworld. The Archon, consumed by the civil war, writes Kentares off. But two wars have left their mark on Ian Dresari — a hard man now, ruling a hard peace. (This leaves the field clear if you ever want to continue into the Black Knight storyline.)

## Branch Notes — if Joanna survived Operation 6
- Joanna serves as civilian head of the transitional government and delivers the political briefing before each operation.
- Operation 8, Mission 4: Joanna insists that Hess stand trial — the capture objective becomes primary, and Ian's darker dialogue is softened.
- Operation 8, Mission 5: Falk surrenders on Joanna's personal guarantee; the hostage phase becomes a timed escort out of the fortress instead of a firefight.
- Karl Sontag remains as Technical Officer and delivers salvage and logistics interludes; Damon Squire remains on the active roster.
