Multiplayer

Five on five.
Thirty seconds to drop.

Pick a mode and you are pooled with everyone who joins in the same thirty seconds. Seats still empty when the counter hits zero are filled by robots, so a match always starts. Matches run on a Cloudflare Durable Object at the edge — no lobby to find, no ports to forward, nothing to install.

  • Deathmatch — five on five, one point per kill, first team to twenty
  • Capture the Flag — turn the flag your colour and points accumulate while you hold it
  • Your six Deployment Bays are your deck, and it freezes at the drop: a frame destroyed in a match is spent
  • Practice Skirmish against bots — offline, instant, no server

Frequently asked questions

Is it really free? What do I need to play?
Yes — completely free, no install, no account required. Any modern desktop browser with WebGL2 (Chrome, Edge, Firefox, Safari) will run it. A keyboard and mouse are strongly recommended.
How do pilot accounts and saves work?
Register a pilot with a callsign and passcode to sync your campaign, hangar and wallet through the cloud registry across devices. Your passcode stays on your device; the browser sends only a derived verifier. Saves are cached locally and sync in the background, so a signed-in pilot can keep playing offline and merge progress after reconnecting. Guest progress lasts only for the lifetime of the tab.
What's playable right now?
All of it: Basic Training (a fully-voiced, input-gated tutorial), all %%FACT:missionCount%% campaign missions from Cold Ignition to Reclamation including the M21 fork, and multiplayer — online five-on-five Deathmatch and Capture the Flag, plus an offline bot skirmish.
How does multiplayer work?
Open Multiplayer from the main menu and join a Deathmatch or Capture the Flag queue. Everyone who joins within the same thirty seconds drops together, five on five, and any seats still empty are filled by robots. Matches run on a Cloudflare Durable Object match server — nothing to configure, nothing to install. There is also an offline Practice Skirmish against bots.
